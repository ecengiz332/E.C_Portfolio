My coding journey began on an average, sleep-deprived night. Motivated by curiosity and a desire to create my skills; I found myself deep in a late-night Youtube rabbit hole researching "how to code for beginners?". I quickly ended up realizing that I needed to take a different approach. I turned to interactive web platforms. My very first coding experience? An HTML page loyal to the art of cooking boiled eggs. It was a humbling experience but, that's how I started to grow my fascination in the field of web development.

Time went on and as I grew my skills I have started transitioning from my self-taught learning to the engineering realms. I got myself enrolled in a college course for web development. That major step is what helped me unlock a deeper understanding of software creation. Today, with a solid command of HTML, and CSS; I started freelancing. I am still growing and learning other programs. As everyone says "coding is always evolving. You continuously learn new things" and that is a step I am willing to take.

I view coding as the art of solving problems and bringing ideas to life. Whether I am writing in HTML, building with CSS or structuring data in Python and logic in JavaScript. I strive on breaking complex problems into manageable pieces. I do not want to just write code that works; I want to be able to understand why it works and how to optimize it. I am incredible proud of how far I have came, and I approach the screen with the same curiosity that started this coding journey— just with much better sleeping habits.
